# 🧠 Mental Models

This directory exists to strengthen my understanding of the concepts I learn throughout my DevSecOps journey.

While completing checkpoints, I often move quickly through new technologies and ideas. Finishing a lab does not automatically mean mastering it, so this folder serves as a reviewer for my future self.

The goal is not to memorize commands, but to understand:

- Why a technology exists.
- What problem it solves.
- How it works internally.
- How it connects to other concepts.
- Where my understanding was incomplete.

---

## 📚 Contents

This directory may contain:

- Mental model corrections
- Concept reviews
- Personal explanations
- Reflections and insights
- Cross-checkpoint connections
- Common mistakes and misunderstandings

---

## 🔄 Review Process

For each topic, I try to answer these questions:

1. What problem does this technology solve?
2. How does it work?
3. What happens behind the scenes?
4. What assumptions did I get wrong?
5. Could I explain it to my past self?

---

## 🎯 Goal

My goal is to build strong foundations instead of collecting tools and buzzwords.

This folder is a reminder that understanding grows through repetition, reflection, and experience.
