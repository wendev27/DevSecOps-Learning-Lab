# Learning Progress

## Overview

This directory is for tracking cybersecurity learning over time: topics studied, practice outcomes, completed labs, recurring mistakes, and follow-up work. It turns observations from [`practice/`](../practice/README.md) into focused next steps.

The current directory contains this guide only. No practice score, lab result, certification, or skill level is claimed here until a dated record documents it.

## What to record

A progress entry may include:

- Date and learning objective.
- Domain or topic.
- Activity completed and its status.
- Expected result and actual result.
- Mistakes, uncertainties, and the reason for each mistake.
- Evidence or references, sanitized of secrets and sensitive data.
- Corrective action and date for the next review.

A future `weak-areas.md` file can track recurring gaps when there is enough information to make that record useful. It is not present yet.

## Recommended workflow

1. Record the outcome of a study or practice session honestly.
2. Group mistakes by domain rather than looking only at an overall score.
3. Prioritize repeated errors and concepts that cannot yet be explained clearly.
4. Revisit the relevant material in [`concepts/`](../concepts/README.md) and [`review/`](../review/README.md).
5. Repeat the exercise and record what changed.
6. Mark an item `Completed` only when the stated objective and evidence support that status; otherwise use `Planned`, `In progress`, or `Needs review`.

## Progress principles

- Track understanding and repeatability, not just completion counts.
- Keep planned work separate from observed results.
- Use consistent terms and scoring when comparing practice attempts.
- Record limitations so that a small lab result is not overgeneralized.
- Reassess weak areas after corrective study.

## Privacy and safety

These records are for personal learning. Remove personal data, credentials, tokens, private keys, confidential system details, and other sensitive information before saving an entry. Keep testing evidence limited to authorized environments and retain only what is necessary to support the learning objective.
