# Learning Progress and Weak-Area Tracking

## Overview

This directory tracks study progress, practice results, completed labs, and cybersecurity topics that require additional attention.

It provides a simple way to evaluate learning over time and make study sessions more focused.

## Purpose

The purpose of this directory is to turn practice results into actionable learning improvements.

Instead of repeatedly studying every topic equally, learners can use their results to identify knowledge gaps, prioritize weak areas, and measure progress through review and reassessment.

## Contents

### `weak-areas.md`

Records topics that need further review.

Suggested information includes:

- Topic or domain.
- Concepts that were misunderstood.
- Common mistakes.
- Planned corrective action.
- Date for the next review.
- Status of the follow-up review.

### Future Progress Records

As the module develops, this directory may also contain:

- Practice examination scores.
- Completed review topics.
- Completed hands-on labs.
- Revision history.
- Self-assessment notes.
- Personal learning milestones.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Record the results of each practice session.
2. Identify incorrect answers and the reason for each mistake.
3. Group mistakes by cybersecurity domain.
4. Prioritize topics with repeated errors or weak conceptual understanding.
5. Review the relevant material in `../Review/` and `../Concepts/`.
6. Repeat the practice exercise and record the outcome.

## Progress Tracking Principles

- Focus on understanding rather than memorizing answer patterns.
- Track recurring mistakes, not just overall scores.
- Reassess weak topics after reviewing them.
- Use consistent scoring methods when comparing practice attempts.
- Update records honestly to maintain a useful picture of progress.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify knowledge gaps early.
- Improve retention through repeated review.
- Measure progress using practice results.
- Build a more focused and sustainable study routine.
- Develop confidence based on demonstrated understanding.

## Privacy

These records are intended for personal learning. Do not commit personal assessment results, confidential recruitment communications, or other sensitive information unless you have a clear reason and permission to share them.
